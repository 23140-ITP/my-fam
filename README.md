# My Fam

## A caring message from home, whenever you need one.

My Fam is a warm, conversational AI companion for the moments when you want a parent’s perspective—but your parent is gone, far away, unavailable, or not someone you can safely call.

It gives you three ways to connect:

- **Mom** for reassurance, empathy, and emotional support.
- **Dad** for a calm, practical point of view.
- **My Fam** for a shared conversation where both perspectives meet.

My Fam also makes care feel actionable. It gently checks in on **food, water, and sleep**, helping you take one small step instead of carrying the whole day alone. When typing is not enough, you can continue the conversation by voice.

## Why we made it

The moments when we miss our parents are often ordinary: deciding what to cook, facing a hard choice, needing encouragement, or realizing we have not eaten all day.

Search can give us information. A generic chatbot can give us an answer. My Fam is designed to give us something different: a caring response, a second perspective, and a gentle nudge toward the next thing that will help.

> You do not have to carry every small thing alone.

## The experience

1. Open My Fam and receive a warm check-in.
2. Complete a small care action for food, water, or sleep.
3. Choose Mom, Dad, or the group chat.
4. Ask for advice, encouragement, or a practical next step.
5. Continue by text or voice.

## A note on what My Fam is—and is not

My Fam is a transparent AI companion. It is not a real parent, a replacement for family or friends, a therapist, a doctor, or an emergency service. It should support real-world connection and professional help—not compete with it.

## Research behind the idea

The product was shaped by public conversations from people who miss everyday conversations with parents, live with emotional distance, need practical life guidance, or want a safe parent-like perspective. See the full [problem, solution, and market report](docs/problem-solution-market-report.md) and the [research evidence board](docs/assets/reddit-research-evidence-board.png).

## Product direction

My Fam is designed to feel like opening a caring message from home: warm cream surfaces, intimate conversation rows, iMessage-inspired bubbles, distinct Mom and Dad accents, calm motion, and a focused iOS-native experience.

## Status

My Fam is a hackathon prototype built to explore how conversational AI can make everyday care feel more human, useful, and emotionally accessible.
